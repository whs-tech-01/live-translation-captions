How it works

  1. Start captions and allow the microphone the first time Chrome asks.
  2. A dark caption bar opens. Drag it to the top of the screen. It stays above everything, including a full-screen slideshow.
  3. Start your slideshow and teach as normal. Captions run about a second behind you.
  4. Hover over the bar for quick controls: text size, English underneath, lighter background.
  5. Come back to this tab and press Stop when you're done.

A headset or lapel mic is much more accurate than the laptop's built-in mic.

Speech is transcribed by Chrome's speech service, the same one Google Slides captions use. Translation runs on this computer; the language model downloads once, then works offline.

Requires Chrome 138 or newer on a laptop or desktop.
