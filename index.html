Set up captions

Speak English into the microphone and translated captions appear in a bar that stays on top of your slides.

How it works

  1. Choose a caption language. Want two on screen? Click Add a second language.
  2. Press Start captions and allow the microphone if Chrome asks.
  3. A caption bar opens. Drag it to the top of the screen — it stays on top of your slideshow.
  4. Teach as normal. Hover over the bar to change text size or show the English underneath.
  5. Press Stop here when you're done.

If the caption window doesn't appear, refresh the page and try again, or check that Chrome has permission to use your microphone (click the icon to the left of the web address).

A headset or lapel mic is much more accurate than the laptop's built-in mic.
